What do these scores mean?
Hybrid Risk

The Hybrid Risk is CryptoDefender's final risk score. It combines the rule-based process analysis with the machine-learning classification and anomaly information.

Hybrid Risk = (Rule Score × 35%) + (ML Score × 50%) + (Anomaly Score × 15%)

A higher Hybrid Risk means the overall evidence is more consistent with cryptocurrency mining.

ML Score

The ML Score is the probability produced by the CNN + LSTM classification part of the hybrid model.

ML Score = Softmax(Classifier Output)[Mining Class]

For example, if the backend returns {{ result.model_score|default:0 }}%, the classifier estimates approximately that level of probability for the mining class.

ML Accuracy

ML Accuracy is not the current computer's mining probability. It represents how accurately the trained model performed on the evaluation data recorded in your metadata.json.

Accuracy = Correct Predictions ÷ Total Evaluation Samples × 100
